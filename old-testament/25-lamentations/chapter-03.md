## 1

**HEB** אֲנִ֤י הַגֶּ֙בֶר֙ רָאָ֣ה עֳנִ֔י בְּשֵׁ֖בֶט עֶבְרָתֽוֹ׃

**JPS** I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of His wrath.

## 2

**JPS** He hath led me and caused me to walk in darkness and not in light.

## 3

**JPS** Surely against me He turneth His hand again and again all the day.

## 4

**GRC** ἐπαλαίωσεν σάρκα μου καὶ δέρμα μου, ὀστέα μου συνέτριψεν.

**JPS** My flesh and my skin hath He worn out; He hath broken my bones.

## 5

**GRC** Βή. Ἀνῳκοδόμησεν κατ᾽ ἐμοῦ, καὶ ἐκύκλωσεν κεφαλήν μου καὶ ἐμόχθησεν,

**HEB** בָּנָ֥ה עָלַ֛י וַיַּקַּ֖ף רֹ֥אשׁ וּתְלָאָֽה׃

**JPS** He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail.

## 6

**JPS** He hath made me to dwell in dark places, as those that have been long dead.

## 7

**GRC** ἀνῳκοδόμησεν κατ᾽ ἐμοῦ καὶ οὐκ ἐξελεύσομαι, ἐβάρυνεν χαλκόν μου.

**ELL** Με περιέφραξε, διά να μη εξέλθω· εβάρυνε τας αλύσεις μου.

**HEB** גָּדַ֧ר בַּעֲדִ֛י וְלֹ֥א אֵצֵ֖א הִכְבִּ֥יד נְחָשְׁתִּֽי׃

**JPS** He hath hedged me about, that I cannot go forth; He hath made my chain heavy.

**GRC** Γίμελ. Καί γε κεκράξομαι καὶ βοήσω, ἀπέφραξεν προσευχήν μου.

**JPS** Yea, when I cry and call for help, He shutteth out my prayer.

**GRC** Δάλεθ. Ἀνῳκοδόμησεν ὁδούς μου, ἐνέφραξεν τρίβους μου, ἐτάραξεν

**JPS** He hath enclosed my ways with hewn stone, He hath made my paths crooked.

## 10

**JPS** He is unto me as a bear lying in wait, as a lion in secret places.

## 11

**GRC** κατεδίωξεν ἀφεστηκότα καὶ κατέπαυσέν με, ἔθετό με ἠφανισμένην.

**JPS** He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces; He hath made me desolate.

**JPS** He hath bent His bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.

## 13

**JPS** He hath caused the arrows of His quiver to enter into my reins.

**HEB** הָיִ֤יתִי שְּׂחֹק֙ לְכָל־עַמִּ֔י נְגִינָתָ֖ם כָּל־הַיּֽוֹם׃

**JPS** I am become a derision to all my people, and their song all the day.

## 15

**GRC** Οὐαύ. Ἐχόρτασέν με πικρίας, ἐμέθυσέν με χολῆς,

**JPS** He hath filled me with bitterness, He hath sated me with wormwood.

**HEB** וַיַּגְרֵ֤ס בֶּֽחָצָץ֙ שִׁנָּ֔י הִכְפִּישַׁ֖נִי בָּאֵֽפֶר׃

**JPS** He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, He hath made me to wallow in ashes.

## 17

**JPS** And my soul is removed far off from peace, I forgot prosperity.

**JPS** And I said: 'My strength is perished, and mine expectation from HaShem.'

## 19

**JPS** Remember mine affliction and mine anguish, the wormwood and the gall.

**JPS** My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is bowed down within me.

**JPS** This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope.

## 25

**JPS** HaShem is good unto them that wait for Him, to the soul that seeketh Him.
